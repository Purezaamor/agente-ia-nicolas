Galicia reported comprehensive net income of ARS 5.3bn, a 49% Q-o-Q decrease and 
implying in ~6% inflation-adjusted ROE – this is below the mid-teens it was printing 
in 2021. Naranja X had a very weak quarter (earnings down almost 100%) and the 
bank unit kept driving results. We see this as a weak quarter – losses from net 
monetary position reached –ARS 37.5bn, up from ARS23-24bn in past quarters, which 
was offset by okay operating income growth. Other than that, NII (ex-securities) 
increased 7% Y-o-Y, due to better NIMs as loans at the bank unit decreased 10% Y-o-
Y. Meanwhile securities and other financial instruments (up +46% Y-o-Y) continue 
driving most of the revenues and balance sheet growth. All in all, a lackluster quarter, 
which in our view is expected given the challenging outlook for banks in Argentina. 
GGAL is trading at 0.8x last reported P/BV (LC).
 Better NIMs not enough to offset soft volumes. NII (ex-securities) came in at 
ARS 29.4bn, up ~7% Y-o-Y but down 28% Q-o-Q. The large sequential decline 
was mostly driven by volumes, while NIMs increased 200bps Q-o-Q and 400bps Y-
o-Y. Meanwhile, bank unit loans to the private sector declined 7% Q-o-Q and 10% 
Y-o-Y. 
 Virtually stable coverage and some improvements in NPLs. Consolidated loan 
loss provisions came in at ARS 6.5bn, down 34% Q-o-Q but roughly doubling Y-o-
Y. This implies in 3% cost of risk, below 4.2% historical avg. (ex-2020). 
Meanwhile, NPLs declined 10bps Q-o-Q to 3.2% (well-above 1Q21 given 
mandatory reliefs polluting base). All in all, coverage stood at 177%, up from 175% 
last quarter and above ~110% pre-pandemic levels (2018-19). 
 Costs down sequentially but efficiency deteriorating on top line. Total non-
interest expenses were stable Y-o-Y and down sequentially. In particular, both 
personnel and administrative expenses declined sequentially, partially offset by 
“other” expenses up 5% Y-o-Y. The company keeps reducing headcount, down by 
27 workers Q-o-Q (-337 in L12M or ~4% of total). Meanwhile, net fees were up 
14% Y-o-Y and down 5% Q-o-Q. Lastly, insurance income was down 10% Q-o-Q 
but recovered 40% Y-o-Y from soft 1Q21. All in all, reported efficiency ratio stood 
at 74% vs. 62% last quarter. 
 Other highlights: (i) inflation-related losses added up ARS 37bn, accelerating from 
ARS 24bn last quarter; (ii) tier 1 ratio came in at 21.4%, virtually stable Q-o-Q – but 
likely not yet reflecting ARS 11bn dividends paid in May; (ii) liquid assets to 
deposits stood at 68%, up ~7p.p. Q-o-Q; (iii) deposits were down 2% Y-o-Y and 6% 
Q-o-Q; (iv) NaranjaX posted only ARS 5mn earnings vs. ARS 1,323mn last quarter, 
impacted by higher inflation losses and provisions; and (v) Galicia transferred 7.4% 
stake in Prisma (acquirer in Argentina) for US$54mn to be paid in 6 years partially 
in peso and US$.
